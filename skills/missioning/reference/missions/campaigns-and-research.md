# Playbook: campaigns, communications and research

## When to use

A marketing campaign or communication from brief to launch and readout, or customer, user or desk
research that informs one. Not for designing a product experience (`experience-design`).

## Skills

- `planning` (marketing domain): brief, goals and backlog of campaign work. Follow its gates.
- `crafting`: each asset (social, email, print, video, presentation) and user research (study
  plans, interview and usability guides, synthesis).
- `synthesizing`: evidence ledgers; `distill` for video sources.
- `campaigning` (planned, wave 3). Fallback: a message house per audience, a channel and calendar
  plan, one asset brief per asset for `crafting`, an approval route per asset, and a funnel that
  shows how the target is reached. Label it "manual, no skill".
- `researching` (planned, wave 3). Fallback: desk research into `synthesizing`'s evidence-ledger
  format; every claim cited with a URL and date, or marked unverified.
- `steering`: the weekly readout, with risks, decision requests and guardrail actions. If not
  installed: a weekly readout of each funnel measure against target.
- Surveys and quantitative analysis in `crafting`: planned enhancement, wave 3. Fallback: script
  every statistic; state the sample size and how it was drawn.

## Steps

1. Charter the mission with `missioning`; Approvers include each outside approver, such as a
   client's brand or compliance reviewer.
2. Run `planning` in the marketing domain to an agreed brief and backlog.
3. Research the audience and market; cite every claim.
4. Write the message house and the channel and calendar plan.
5. Plan each outside approver's decision date and one resubmission inside the deadline.
6. Brief and produce each asset with `crafting`.
7. Get each outside approval on the final file, not a draft.
8. Launch only after the launch gate; read back what was published.
9. Read the funnel measures against target; file lessons; close out.

Milestones: M1 brief and research (steps 2 to 4), M2 assets and approvals (5 to 7), M3 launch
and readout (8 and 9).

## Human gates

- Kickoff: the charter, open questions with defaults, and the effects to pre-authorize.
- Brief and message house: `planning`'s sign-off by the named approvers.
- Each external approval: Source `written:` or `tracker:`, on the final file.
- Launch: an outside send of the exact plan of what goes where and when; pre-authorized at
  kickoff for the calendar plan the approvers sign, or asked when it arises.

The post-campaign readout is the owner's review of the close-out; it blocks nothing.

## Definition of Done

Every published asset has its approval recorded on the final file, not a draft; the funnel's
measures have readings against target; lessons are filed.

## Red flags

- A finished asset that skipped its approver.
- No time left for a resubmission before the deadline.
- A market number with no source.
- A post or email sent without the launch gate.
- Content the approved brief excludes, added on a chat request without a reason 1 stop.
