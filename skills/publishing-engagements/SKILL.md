# Publishing engagements

Prepare the selected audience's next decision from reviewed evidence. An exported file is not
delivery, business acceptance or permission to share.

## Bind the shared engagement first

Load `blueprinting` through Amp's skill tool, even on direct entry. Bind to the coordinator base
returned by that tool, not this skill's directory, the engagement directory or a nearby checkout.
Read `reference/composition.md` at that base and follow its dependency and explicit-root checks
before using any helper. If resolution fails or is ambiguous, stop the affected work; do not
search for another installation or install one implicitly. Keep native Amp routing.

## Prepare, inspect and hand off only what was selected

Read the coordinator's `reference/publication.md` before building a content packet and
`reference/exports.md` before exporting. Use `reference/upkeep.md` for external-effect recovery.
Reuse crafting for narrative and visual judgment. Choose the documents needed for the journey,
not every available format. Do not copy internal reflections into speaker notes.

Bind content to exact source revisions, audience, destination and reported review. Carry inherited
restrictions and caveats into all formats. Keep formal specifications detailed and decks concise
without losing business meaning. Choose SCQA, recommendation-first, options/trade-offs or process
walkthrough according to the reader's decision. Never invent outcomes to complete the story.

Return the reviewed packet, actual inspected exports and explicit limitations. Check editability,
readability and consistency; hashes identify content, not truth. Current helpers support generic
Markdown, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, PDF and selected exact-byte ZIPs; tailored branding remains under development.
For XLSX estimates, test input edits, native recalculation and invalid-state recovery; cached
totals alone are not verification. Edited files are unreviewed scenarios, not a changed baseline.
For ZIPs, use the staged-candidate and named-inspection workflow in `reference/exports.md`;
never regenerate inspected files or archive a directory. Exported does not mean delivered.
Use only authorized destinations; inspect uncertain effects before retrying. Do not activate
cloud connections, install branding assets or send client communications implicitly.
