# Designing functions

Turn evidence into a challengeable functional specification and an explicit next business decision,
not a technical architecture or permission to operate a client's ERP.

## Bind the shared engagement first

Load `blueprinting` through Amp's skill tool, even on direct entry. Bind to the coordinator base
returned by that tool, not this skill's directory, the engagement directory or a nearby checkout.
Read `reference/composition.md` at that base and follow its dependency and explicit-root checks
before using any helper. If resolution fails or is ambiguous, stop the affected work; do not
search for another installation or install one implicitly. Keep native Amp routing.

## Design and test the business contract

Read the coordinator's `reference/functional-design.md`; add `reference/decisions.md` for agreement
and `reference/proposals.md` for fit-gap or estimates. Use `reference/discovery.md` to identify
missing evidence rather than filling it with plausible requirements.

Map goals to actors, concerns, responsibilities, processes, rules, exceptions, receiving handoffs
and observable acceptance. Keep as-is evidence distinct from to-be recommendations. Compare
options and their effects on different stakeholders. Include absent receiving teams and unknowns.
Technical questions become explicit dependencies for the appropriate specialist.

Return the functional baseline or labelled draft, traceable requirements, authority/coverage gaps,
alternatives and next decision. A structural coverage pass is not semantic completeness or client
acceptance. Integrate child findings against the exact parent intent and criteria; unresolved
child gaps cannot disappear behind a done flag. At kickoff compare agreement with current reality,
including changed assumptions, actual owners and readiness, rather than replaying discovery.
