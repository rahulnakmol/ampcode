# Campaigns, communications and research mission

A marketing campaign or communication from brief to launch and readout, or customer, user or desk
research that informs one. Playbook:
[`campaigns-and-research.md`](../../skills/missioning/reference/missions/campaigns-and-research.md).
[All missions](README.md).

## Use it when

- a launch, announcement or campaign needs a brief, assets, outside approvals and a readout;
- you need cited desk research or user research (interview guides, synthesis) for a decision.

Not for designing a product experience (use [UX and UI design](experience-design.md)).

## Start it

```text
Use missioning to run a campaign mission for [launch or message] to [audience]
by [date]. I decide; [name, role] approves brand and [name, role] approves
compliance. Pre-authorize posting the calendar plan the approvers sign.
```

Before drafting, the agent asks you once for: the decision the work informs, the dates, who
counts as the audience, pilot or sample size, house rules, and the budget. You then approve, once,
the charter, open questions with defaults and the effects to pre-authorize.

## How it runs

| Milestone | What happens | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 Brief and research | Brief and backlog in planning's marketing domain; audience and market research with every claim cited; message house and channel plan | `planning`, `synthesizing`, `distill` for video sources |
| M2 Assets and approvals | Approver dates with one resubmission inside the deadline; each asset briefed and produced; each outside approval on the final file | `crafting` |
| M3 Launch and readout | Launch after the launch gate and read back what was published; funnel measures against target; lessons filed | `steering` |

`campaigning` and `researching` are planned. Until they exist the agent writes the message house,
channel plan and asset briefs, and the desk research in `synthesizing`'s evidence-ledger format,
labelled "manual, no skill". Every statistic is scripted, with its sample size stated.

## Gates you decide

- Kickoff (above).
- The brief and message house: `planning`'s sign-off by the named approvers.
- Each outside approval, written or in the tracker, on the final file, not a draft.
- Launch: what goes where and when, pre-authorized for the signed calendar plan or asked.

## Done when

Every published asset has its approval recorded on the final file, the funnel's measures have
readings against target, and lessons are filed.

## Example

**PostgreSQL 19 launch campaign** (validation run 4a, scores 8/7/9). The kickoff brief was
approved at its sixth revision. The agent refused to say "available now" for an unreleased
version and raised a stop on a LinkedIn teaser the brief did not cover. In another run, Bitwarden
passkey feedback (4b), the agent added forum handles to the steering summary after a casual chat
request; the playbook now treats content the approved brief excludes as a change to the baseline,
which needs a stop.
