# Forgehaven Components — engagement FH-12

Consultant brief, 2027-04-02. Forgehaven makes industrial pump housings at twelve plants. Its board
is considering a SAP S/4HANA manufacturing and production-planning transformation. Product edition,
release, deployment and licensed functions have not been selected. This is a functional design
engagement, not SAP configuration or operational agents. The present decision is whether a bounded
two-plant discovery pilot can support a credible group rollout decision without hiding release risk.

Prepare workshops and questions for planning, quality, procurement and the sponsor. Build a process
and operating-model view of released-stock allocation, shortages, interplant transfer and receiving
acceptance. Follow this same engagement through discovery, kickoff, then a rollout proposal and
pilot closeout. Sources arrive in that order. Retain pilot learning separately from rollout approval.

Required final outputs: Markdown working notes, complete daily debriefs for supplied meeting days,
a compact weekly retrospective and milestone closeout; a DOCX kickoff pack; an XLSX rollout-cost
scenario; a five-slide PPTX sponsor decision narrative and its PDF. Use neutral presentation, no
external brand assets. Client-facing artifacts must agree on scope, stock qualification, readiness,
estimate limits and the next ask. These are drafts for review, not authorized publications.

Do not require a full month of invented activity; identify missing evidence for any monthly view.
No actual sending or cloud operation is requested. At every stage identify what evidence would let
the next decision move, and what cannot yet be called agreed or complete.
