# Northwater Library Consortium — RFP NLC-31

2027-10-04. Your consultancy is considering a response to a library service-management tender.
Prepare a clarification questionnaire, an obligation-by-obligation response and a bid recommendation.
The buyer seeks defensible commitments, not an unqualified “yes” to everything. Keep addenda and
the actual authority to waive requirements separate from sales opinion. No external research,
submission, certification purchase or client contact is authorized in this exercise.

Required outputs: a Markdown obligation/evidence ledger and a DOCX draft response with exceptions,
owners, commercial assumptions and the next decision. A no-bid or conditional draft may be useful
if mandatory evidence is absent; no final conforming bid may pretend a missing proof exists.
