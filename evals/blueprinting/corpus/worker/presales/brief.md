# Aster Learning Network — pre-sales ALN-2

2027-02-11. A network of tutoring centres is considering an adviser-facing enrolment service.
Prepare questions and a defensible discovery-first offer, not an RFP compliance submission. The
buyer must choose whether to investigate a process change, trial a new platform, or retain the
current service with improvements. Understand adviser effort, consent, failed bookings and finance
handoffs. No vendor/version has been established as suitable.

Produce a Markdown opportunity record, an XLSX cost-range scenario and a short DOCX proposal with
scope, exclusions, acceptance, assumptions and next buying decision. It must be useful if the
opportunity is paused or lost. Do not send an offer, invent a price, or turn a buyer's interest into
acceptance. There is no requirement for slides or PDF in this case.
