- P04.a: EUR internal cost rates dated 2027-02-11. Discovery: 3 centres × 2–3 person-days × EUR800;
  prototype: 1 × 5–8 person-days × EUR900; validation: 3 centres × 1–2 person-days × EUR700.
  Cost contingency is 10% of base. These are planning assumptions, not a supplier quotation.
- P04.b: Prototype follows discovery; validation follows prototype. Consent/legal review, migration,

  unknown; no calendar duration follows just by dividing person-days. The offer is not a full rollout.
