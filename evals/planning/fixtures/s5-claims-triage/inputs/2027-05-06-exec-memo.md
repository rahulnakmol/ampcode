# Memo: AI-assisted claims triage (Elena Petrova, Chief Claims Officer, Northwind Mutual)

Motor first notice of loss (FNOL) volume is 410,000 claims a year. Average time from FNOL to first decision on liability and route (fast-track, standard, complex, fraud review) is 4.6 days. Customers who wait more than 3 days for first contact are 2.3x more likely to complain (2026 NPS study). Handler headcount is flat and attrition is 24%.

I want an AI assistant that reads the FNOL call, photos, and policy, then proposes the route and a reserve estimate within one hour, with a handler confirming. Goal: first decision within 1 day for 80% of claims by Q2 2028, without increasing complaints or fraud leakage.

I am not asking for automatic decisions on claims. A person stays accountable.

Budget envelope for year 1: EUR 1.8M. Board update in September.
