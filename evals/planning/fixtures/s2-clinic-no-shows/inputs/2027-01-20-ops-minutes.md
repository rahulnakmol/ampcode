# Minutes: Outpatient no-shows working group, 20 January 2027
St Aldric Health Trust. Attendees: Grace Mensah (Director of Operations, chair), Dr Samuel Okoro (Clinical Lead, Outpatients), Helen Park (Booking Office Manager), Ravi Desai (Digital Services), Joan Whitley (Patient Advisory Group)

1. Size of the problem
- Outpatient did-not-attend (DNA) rate 14.2% over last 12 months (target set by the regional board: 8% by March 2028).
- About 61,000 missed appointments a year. Finance estimates each costs about £120.
- Worst clinics: dermatology (19%), physiotherapy (17%), diabetes (16%).

2. Causes discussed
- Letters arrive late or not at all; 22% of patients said they never got the letter (2025 survey).
- No easy way to cancel or rebook: phone line is busy 9-11am, average wait 14 minutes.
- Some appointments booked 9+ months ahead; patients forget.

3. Ideas
- SMS reminders 7 days and 2 days before, with a link to confirm, cancel, or rebook. Ravi: the SMS gateway contract already exists (GovNotify-style), cost about 2p per message.
- Fill cancelled slots from a waiting list.
- Weekly DNA report per clinic for clinic managers.

4. Concerns
- Joan: many older patients don't use smartphones; any change must not make it harder for them. Keep phone and letter.
- Dr Okoro: some clinics (sexual health, mental health) must not send appointment details by SMS for privacy. Content must be neutral.
- Helen: booking team is already 2 people short; a new process cannot add workload.

5. Decisions
- Pilot in dermatology and physiotherapy first.
- Information governance sign-off needed before any SMS goes out (Data Protection Officer: Mark Ellis).
