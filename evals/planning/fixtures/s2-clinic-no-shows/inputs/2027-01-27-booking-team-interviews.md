# Interview notes (Helen Park, Booking Office) - 27 Jan 2027

- Booking staff spend about 30% of their day handling rebook calls.
- When a patient cancels, the slot usually goes empty because nobody has time to call the waiting list.
- The patient admin system (PAS) can export appointments nightly as CSV. It cannot receive updates automatically; changes are keyed in by hand. Ravi says an integration would take 6+ months and needs the PAS vendor.
- Staff would prefer a daily list of "confirmed / cancelled / no reply" to work through, not more phone calls.
- Patients often call to say "I got a text, is this real?" when other hospitals started SMS. A clear sender name and a phone number in the text helped there.
