## Evidence and interpretation

Eight fresh Task contexts received prompt-only cases and either the previous package snapshot
from `fab9547c44db1135747aad06e89527822a7c936f` or the revised package. Each could read its package
references; only the inventory case received source fixtures. All produced simulated responses;
none executed the proposed workflow or delegated nested reviews. The subject restriction is
identical across versions. A separate judge received anonymous A/B labels, prompts, fixtures and
the frozen checks, not the skill source or version mapping. The parent inspected all responses
and judgments and agreed with the dispositions. No skill repair was needed after these runs.

The previous payment response did not literally say "fresh" or "read-only", but the judge accepted
its independent, packet-based inspection/reporting assignment as satisfying the behavior. That
interpretation is retained rather than tightening the rubric after seeing which version won.
The revised export response proposes seven-day retention, while the old one recommends deferring
to a policy owner; neither is an adopted decision or evidence of the right real-world policy.

See `judgments.json` for every check and quoted evidence; `manifest.json` maps anonymous labels
to variants and records word counts, verbatim-output hashes and evaluated skill-file hashes.
The eight `*-old_skill.md` / `*-with_skill.md` files preserve raw responses. Inputs and the frozen
checks are in `../scenarios.json`. No final behavioral instruction edits followed evaluation.

## Limits

- The rubric intentionally tests the new packet-isolation requirement; its one gained check is
  evidence of instruction uptake, not an independent measure of reduced customer incidents.
- Same inherited runtime, one response per case/version, one judge, public synthetic fixtures;
  no significance test, cross-model replication, trigger-rate measurement or latency claim.
- Task did not return timing or token totals. They are unavailable, not zero; no cost saving or
  exact budget-compliance claim follows from these runs.
- Source inspection caught the inventory defect; preservation tests were proposed, not executed.
  No real payment provider, storage backend, Goldfish dispatch chain or protected merge was tested.
- The full video was not watched or transcribed. Its detailed publisher description informed the
  inventory scenario; the primary article and repository ground the adaptation.
- Package validation and native PR CI are separate checks. This evaluation does not waive the
  outstanding live mission/release qualification or authorize Personal publication from dev.
