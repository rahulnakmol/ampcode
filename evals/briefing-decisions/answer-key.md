# Answer key: the four novice questions

Written before any reader ran. The judge grades each answer against these points, with a quote
from the reader's answer. An answer is correct when it has every required point and no wrong one.

1. **What am I approving?** Moving the sales demo website from the office computer to a paid
   hosting service.
2. **What can go wrong?** Required: the site may be offline for up to one hour during the move;
   old links in sent emails stop working after 30 days. (Naming the monthly cost as a risk is
   allowed.)
3. **What does it cost?** Required: 25 US dollars each month. (Naming the reader's own time is
   allowed.)
4. **What is not included?** Required: the main company website; customer data; a custom domain
   name.
